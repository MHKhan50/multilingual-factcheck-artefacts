# Ethics, safety, and responsible use

- Do not treat a source report as ground truth.
- Do not publish allegations about identifiable private people.
- Do not infer protected traits, intent, legal guilt, or responsibility.
- Preserve attribution, URL, publication time, language, and source-family metadata.
- Do not count syndicated copies as independent corroboration.
- Treat casualty figures as attributed estimates and preserve disagreement.
- Human review is mandatory for consequential use.
- Do not use this tool for targeting, surveillance, operational military decisions, or violence facilitation.
- Exclude graphic imagery and personal data from the initial benchmark.
- Respect dataset terms, source copyright, robots/access restrictions, and redistribution rules.
